This release makes the transfer of large files recover by itself when the Remote Desktop control loses a message on the way to the session.

## Why

Since 0.4.0, some transfers failed at their very start with a message lost on the way. I read the logs of both sides each time, four times. On 9 November with 0.4.0, the transferBegin message of 101 characters never reached the agent; a minute later, calls of about 350 000 characters never arrived at all, which is why the client has kept every call under 200 000 characters since 0.4.3; later that day with 0.4.1, the fileBegin message of about 200 characters went missing 8 ms before a call of 174 868 characters; on 10 November with 0.5.0, the call that held transferBegin and fileBegin, 314 characters, vanished 8 ms before a call of 140 081 characters. In the three other cases, a short call to the Remote Desktop control followed within about 10 ms by a large one was lost in its entirety, with no error on either side, while the calls before and after it arrived intact. Keeping the strings alive and grouping the messages in 0.4.3 did not prevent it. Since the cause is inside the control, the transfer now has to cope with it.

## What the transfer does now

- Every message of a transfer carries its number. The agent puts the messages back in order, and when one is missing it asks the client for it, again every half second while it does not come, up to ten times.
- The client keeps what it sent until the agent confirms it, and sends the missing messages again when asked. When it waits for a confirmation that does not come, it sends its last message again after two seconds, so that a loss at the very end is found too.
- The client sends the short messages together with the next large one, in one call, which should make the loss itself rare.
- Both logs count the messages sent again in the summary of each transfer. "A message of the transfer was lost on the way" should no longer appear.

## Upgrade

Install 0.5.1 on both sides. With an older agent, the 0.5.1 client sends the messages as 0.5.0 did, without numbers, and a lost message still fails the transfer.
